# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct · 贡献者公约行为准则

> English · 中文双语

## Our Pledge · 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

我们作为成员、贡献者和领导者，承诺让每个参与我们社区的人都免受骚扰，无论其年龄、
体型、显性或隐性残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会
经济地位、国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何。

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

我们承诺以促进一个开放、热情、多元、包容、健康的社区的方式行动和互动。

## Our Standards · 我们的标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment 有助于营造积极环境的行为示例：

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people 对他人表现出同理心与善意
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences 尊重不同的意见、观点与经历
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback 给予并优雅地接受建设性反馈
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes 承担责任并向受我们错误影响的人道歉
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community 关注不仅对个人、而且对整个社区最有利的事

Examples of unacceptable behavior 不可接受的行为示例：

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances 使用性化语言或图像，以及性关注或挑逗
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- Public or private harassment 公开或私下骚扰
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission 未经明确许可发布他人隐私信息
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting 其他在职业场合可合理视为不当的行为

## Enforcement Responsibilities · 执行责任

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

社区领导者负责澄清和执行我们可接受行为的标准，并会对其认为不当、具有威胁性、
冒犯性或有害的行为采取适当且公正的纠正措施。

## Scope · 适用范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

本行为准则适用于所有社区空间，也适用于个人在公共空间正式代表社区的情形。

## Enforcement · 执行

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

可向负责执行的社区领导者举报任何辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为。所有投诉都将得到
及时、公正的审查与调查。
